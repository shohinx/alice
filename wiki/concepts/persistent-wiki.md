# Persistent Wiki Pattern

This repository is designed as a persistent, compounding knowledge base rather than a one-shot retrieval store.

## Summary

The important idea is to maintain a synthesized wiki between the user and the raw sources. When new material arrives, the wiki is updated, cross-references are refreshed, contradictions are noted, and useful answers are filed back into the repository.

## Why It Matters

- Knowledge accumulates instead of being re-derived from scratch.
- Cross-references and summaries stay current.
- New sources can revise older claims instead of living beside them in isolation.
- Answers from investigation become durable artifacts.
